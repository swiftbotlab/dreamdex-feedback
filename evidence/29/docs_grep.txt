  6253	## Take profit and stop loss
  6254	
  6255	A **take profit (TP)** closes a position when the price reaches your target, and a **stop loss (SL)** closes it when the price moves against you. Both are reduce-only.
  6256	
  6257	- Set them on a new order, or on an open position from the positions table.
  6258	- On a limit order, TP/SL can be armed before it fills only with GTC or ALO, the time-in-force options that rest on the book.
  6259	- They show as lines on the chart, where you can drag them to a new price or cancel them.
  6260	
  6261	## TP and SL hold a little STT
  6262	
  6263	Each pending TP or SL holds **0.15 STT** from your wallet. It pays for the transaction that fires the trigger, and you get it back if you cancel it. Keep enough STT in the wallet for every TP and SL you want open.
  6264
