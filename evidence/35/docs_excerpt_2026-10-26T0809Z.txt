The two roundings differ and are not interchangeable : unrealized PnL truncates toward zero, while funding ceils toward +∞ so a payer never underpays. For a negative quotient that is the opposite of rounding the magnitude up. accruedFunding uses the pool's projected cumulative index, so it includes intervals no one has settled yet — settlement is permissionless and lazy, and measuring against the settled index under-reports what the account already owes.
